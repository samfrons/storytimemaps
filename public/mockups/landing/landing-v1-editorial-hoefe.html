The Project

Preserving Memory Through Data

This interactive map documents over 10,000 Jewish-owned businesses that once thrived in Berlin before the systematic persecution began.

Using archival research from the Humboldt University Berlin, we have reconstructed the locations, ownership histories, and eventual fates of these enterprises—from small family shops to major department stores.

Watch History Unfold

Drag the timeline slider to see how the business landscape changed month by month from 1900 to 1945.

Each marker represents a real business. Colors indicate their status at that moment in time—active, struggling under persecution, or closed. Watch as the vibrant commercial district gradually empties.
