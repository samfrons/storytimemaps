Theme Comparison

The Moody Dark Theme uses Space Mono typography with warm peach/coral accents on a dark purple-gray background. It creates a modern, editorial feel suited for tech-savvy audiences.

The Hackesche Hoefe Theme uses Playfair Display (serif) + Inter (sans-serif) with a cream background and steel blue/mint/amber accents. Inspired by the historic Hackesche Hoefe website, it creates an elegant, archival aesthetic appropriate for historical documentation and museum contexts.
