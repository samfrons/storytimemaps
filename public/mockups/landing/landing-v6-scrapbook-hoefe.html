Every Record Tells a Story

Behind each entry in this database lies a family, a dream, a legacy systematically erased.

From department stores on Alexanderplatz to small workshops in the Scheunenviertel, we have documented the addresses, owners, and ultimate fates of businesses that once defined Berlin's commercial landscape. Each record is backed by archival research from Humboldt University Berlin.
